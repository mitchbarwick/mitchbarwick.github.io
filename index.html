01 — Practice

Onions have layers.
So do I.

Design and research matter at every layer of an organisation. The work moves between all three, and AI now speeds up each of them.

i

Strategic

Deciding which problems deserve attention. Jobs‑to‑be‑done research, innovation frameworks, and roadmaps built around high‑value customer problems.

ii

Structural

Connecting journeys, systems and teams. Service blueprints, design systems, content strategy, and AI‑assisted workflows that make delivery faster.

iii

Surface

Building the thing itself. Prototypes, agents and production interfaces, made alongside engineers, analysts and, increasingly, AI as a pair.

02 — Selected work

Things I’ve
shaped.

Highlights from leading design at Greater Bank, a customer‑owned bank rebuilding its entire digital platform (2019–2022). Clipchamp stories are next.

01

Digital transformation

Platforms · iOS · Android · Web

Rebuilt every digital platform, from the core banking system and staff‑facing tools to the iOS, Android and web apps.

02

Design system & content strategy

Systems · Speed

One system across web, iOS and Android, used internally and by external partners to build consistent, high‑quality interfaces at speed.

03

Innovation framework

Strategy · Prioritisation

“Where should we innovate?” A framework that gathers ideas from across the business and scores them on desirability, uniqueness and brand fit. It then prioritises what the digital team can deliver.

04

Levers of satisfaction

Research · Kano

Kano analysis of 15 candidate features for a secure messaging service. Response time came out as the biggest driver of satisfaction, which kept the scope to what really delights.

05

The countdown clock

Research · Product

Research showed customers want banks to help them clear loans faster. A live payoff estimate shows the impact of every extra repayment.

06

Inclusive by design

Accessibility · WCAG 2.0 AA

Every product built toward WCAG 2.0 AA, and beta apps tested with low and no vision users.
