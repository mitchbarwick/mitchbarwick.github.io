## 4. Greater Bank (richest public record)

Source for most of this: the June 2020 Newy Tech People interview (`PUBLIC`).

- Identified as Principal Designer by June 2020, previously UX Designer there.
- Described the Principal role as support for the wider design organisation: hands-on UI and prototyping, bringing clarity to upcoming epics before teams pick them up, improving process, connecting design downstream to engineering and upstream to product and strategy, coaching designers.
- **UX as a practice did not exist there before this work.** Over about a year the team established UX and how design flows into software delivery. Strong candidate for a historical case study: *establishing product design inside a traditional financial organisation.*
- Team shape in 2020: UX designers, dedicated design research, content/brand design, exploration/service design, working alongside engineering rather than under it. Argument: research is a first-class discipline, not a bolt-on to UX.
- **The persona story:** research surveyed roughly 1,000–1,200 people across NSW on attitudes and behaviours to money and built six personas. About 50 people from the internal product/technology group took the same survey and nearly all clustered on one persona. Three customer segments weren't represented internally at all. Lesson: internal opinion can't substitute for research. Good short essay.
- **Customers in the sprint:** ran five-day design sprints and experimented with customers sketching concepts alongside the team before the market validated them. Participatory design, early.
- **30 / 60 / 90:** show work at 30% (enough to communicate the idea), 60% (direction clearer), 90% (essentially resolved, last 10% spent helping engineering deliver). Origin: as a younger designer he hid work until polished, then realised that was backwards. The earlier people see it, the cheaper it is to learn the idea is wrong.
- Design philosophy from the interview: design is facilitation, communication, empathy and connecting disciplines, not just producing artefacts. Good design makes engineering faster by clarifying the customer need, the outcome that matters and what to build.
- Hiring (public job ads): Product Designers to grow the design system, lift UI quality, emphasise accessibility, and design straightforward experiences across iOS, Android and web. `PUBLIC`
- 2020 prediction: mixed remote/in-office teams could be harder than fully remote ones, because remote people become second-class participants. `SELF`

Already on the site under Selected work: digital transformation, design system and content strategy, innovation framework, Kano "levers of satisfaction", the countdown clock, inclusive by design (WCAG 2.0 AA). `SITE`

## 8. Recurring themes

3. **Connecting strategy to delivery.** Operates between product strategy and the people shipping.
4. **High craft without preciousness.** Show early, learn, ship, observe, refine (30/60/90, MVP mindset).
5. **Humans over process.** Dedicated research, customers in sprints, candid and kind leadership, career development, remote inclusion.

Three-line promise: *I can design. I can build design teams. I can help organisations work out what design needs to become next.*
